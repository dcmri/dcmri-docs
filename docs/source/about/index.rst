About

-------
History
-------

``dcmri`` is developed and maintained by `miblab.org <https://miblab.org/>`_ in the `Medical Imaging Group <https://www.sheffield.ac.uk/smph/research/themes/imaging>`_ of the University of Sheffield, UK. The framework was developed originally as part of the `TRISTAN project <https://www.ihi.europa.eu/projects-results/project-factsheets/tristan>`_, and continues to be developed with support from the `Sheffield Biomedical Research Centre <https://www.sheffieldbrc.nihr.ac.uk/>`_. ``dcmri`` integrates functionality developed for TRISTAN and other projects, and includes translations of older code originally `implemented in IDL <https://sites.google.com/site/plaresmedima/home>`_.
